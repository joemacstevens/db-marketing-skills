## Top 2: cut and ready to audition

### 1. RHYNO (Travis Scott) instrumental
- Full: `candidates/rhyno-travis-scott-instrumental-full.mp3` (3:00)
- BPM **118.9** (aubiotrack, 1 bar = 2.018s). Very compressed, flat loop at about -9 dB RMS. One stripped breakdown in the middle, **87.2s to 98.2s source**: hats out, bass dips, then near silence for 97.3 to 98.1. The full beat slams back at **98.2s source**. A second breakdown starts at 160.5 (outro).
- **Window A (recommended): source 76.0 to 106.0** → `candidates/rhyno-window-A-76.0-106.0.mp3`
  - 0.0s: full beat from frame 0, under the opener, title card and first VO lines
  - 11.2s: beat strips to the breakdown, where the key VO line sits on a bare bed
  - 21.3 to 22.1s: near-silent gap, which can take one last VO word or nothing
  - **22.2s: slam (drop)** into the VO-free montage to 30s
- **Window B (alt): source 96.2 to 126.2** → `candidates/rhyno-window-B-96.2-126.2.mp3`. One bar of the breakdown tail is the lead-in, the **drop lands at 2.0s** (on the title card), then 28s of steady drive. No second lift, so the montage lift comes from un-ducking the bed when the VO ends.

### 2. AH HA (Cardi B) official instrumental
- Full: `candidates/ah-ha-cardi-b-instrumental-full.mp3` (2:56)
- BPM **~96** (aubiotrack, 0.626s per beat, 1 bar = 2.50s; `aubio tempo` reads 105, which is off). Loud hook sections around -7 dB alternate with sparse 808 verses around -17 dB, and every section ends on a hard dead stop.
- **Window: source 98.9 to 128.9** → `candidates/ah-ha-window-98.9-128.9.mp3`
  - 0.0 to 2.5s: one bar of the full hook under the opener and title
  - 2.5s: drops to the sparse 808 verse (bass + kick, hats build from about 12s), a natural VO bed. There's a short stop at about 12.0s that works as a VO beat
  - 19.2s: bass drops out (pre-drop), then a dead stop at **21.9 to 22.5s**
  - **22.5s: slam (source 121.41)** into a full-energy VO-free montage to 30s
- Tradeoff vs RHYNO: AH HA has the better built-in structure (quiet verse, then slam), but RHYNO is the fresher and more culturally loud pick, and darker.

## Weekly series: one track per week or a signature bed?

**One trending instrumental per week, inside a fixed signature structure.** "Trending" goes stale in about 7 days, so the song should rotate. What viewers should recognize every Monday is the shape: beat on frame 0, VO, stripped breakdown under the key line, slam into the montage, plus the same end card. Use `fallback-bed.mp3` (or a regenerated house bed) as the standing default for weeks when nothing trending fits the dark/dramatic brief.
